## About

I hold a degree in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, and I have close to two years of
hands-on experience running ERP-based order, inventory and e-commerce operations — meaning I
have personally worked the kind of processes I now analyse.

I am building my career in **business analysis**. The case studies on this profile exist to
demonstrate my requirements-analysis and process-modelling skills through concrete artefacts:
end-to-end documentation from stakeholder interviews to BRDs, user stories and UAT scenarios.

I am currently working through a data science bootcamp, deepening the SQL and Power BI side of
my toolkit alongside it.

## Focus areas

| Area | What I do |
|---|---|
| **Requirements analysis** | Stakeholder and impact analysis, interview design, translating vague requests into measurable requirements, BRD authoring, business rules (BR) and functional/non-functional requirements (FR/NFR) |
| **Process modelling** | As-is / to-be swimlane flows, root-cause annotation, identifying inefficiencies and hand-off breakdowns, improvement proposals |
| **Agile documentation** | Epic decomposition, user stories with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria, dependency and priority management |
| **Testing & validation** | UAT scenarios (happy path / alternative / negative), test data set design, requirement–story–test traceability matrices |
| **Data analysis** | SQL querying, visualisation and reporting with Power BI |
